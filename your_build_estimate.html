WHAT YOUR ESTIMATE INCLUDES

🌲

47mm treated thermowood walls. Heat dried at 200°C with up to 96% of the moisture removed, or pinewood if you prefer.

🔩

Stainless steel tightening bands and fixings throughout, with double glazed windows and sandglass door options.

🧰

Everything needed included: benches with backrests, structural floor panels, lockable solid timber door, heater surround with fire protection, drainage, ventilation and volcanic stones.

Made to last a lifetime

We are a family run business. We hand build in our workshop down in Wales, and our products are built to last a lifetime.

Not a kit. Handmade from the ground up

We are not a kit sauna company that imports standard units. Every build leaves our workshop made to order, right down to our purpose built trailers, and everything needed to use it from day one is in your estimate.
