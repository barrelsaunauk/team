Venue Sauna Agreement

A mobile sauna placed at a wedding venue, booked by guests through the Barrel Sauna UK system.

Barrel Sauna UK

Thermal Living Limited, trading as Barrel Sauna UK

Referred to as "BSUK"

The venue

Venue name and address

Referred to as "the Venue"

Dated date

  1. Purpose

    BSUK places a mobile sauna at the Venue. The Venue offers sauna access to wedding guests as an add-on to its wedding packages, and every booking is made through the BSUK booking system.

  2. The sauna

    The sauna is model and size. It remains the property of BSUK at all times. Its position on the Venue's grounds is agreed in writing before delivery and is not moved without BSUK's agreement.

  3. What BSUK provides

    At no cost to the Venue, BSUK provides:

    • delivery and setup of the sauna;
    • the booking system, including guest booking and payment;
    • a digital key lock, so guests can only use the sauna during their booked time;
    • maintenance of the sauna.
  4. What the Venue provides
    • A solid, level base for the sauna, and access for delivery, maintenance and collection.
    • Power to the sauna if needed: power and running costs.
    • The sauna included as an option in its wedding packages and promoted to couples.
    • No bookings or payments for the sauna taken outside the BSUK system.
  5. Bookings and payment

    All bookings and payments go through the BSUK system. Each payment is split automatically between the Venue and BSUK.

    • Price per wedding: per wedding amount
    • Split between the Venue and BSUK: split
    • When each party's share is paid: payout timing
  6. Showcase

    The sauna also serves as a showcase for BSUK in the area. By arrangement with the Venue, BSUK may photograph and film the sauna on site and bring prospective customers to see it, at times that do not clash with weddings.

  7. Use and safety

    BSUK supplies the safety rules for using the sauna. The Venue makes sure guests are given them and that the sauna is only used during booked times.

  8. Damage, insurance and transport
    • Damage to the sauna while at the Venue: who carries it
    • Insurance of the sauna and public liability for guest use: who carries it
    • Transport to and from the Venue: who carries it
  9. Term

    This agreement starts on start date and runs for term, then continues until either party ends it under clause 11.

  10. Removal of the sauna

    BSUK may remove the sauna on notice period written notice. BSUK may remove it straight away if bookings or payments for the sauna are taken outside the BSUK system.

  11. Ending the agreement

    Either party may end this agreement with notice period written notice. BSUK then collects the sauna, and the Venue gives access for collection. Bookings already paid for are honoured or refunded.

  12. Guest information

    Guest booking details are held in the BSUK system and used only to manage sauna bookings.

  13. Liability

    Liability terms, for solicitor review

  14. Law

    This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales.
