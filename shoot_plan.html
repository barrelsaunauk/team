What we're filming on Wednesday, and why

This month is all about building a library of B-roll and stills of every configuration we make, so we have it ready for brochures, sales calls and the gallery.

Our builds come in so many variations, and understanding them is what leads to the sale. Our salespeople need to understand it, and so does the person buying.
